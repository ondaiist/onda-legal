Onda Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Onda iOS app ("Onda", "we", "us") handles your information. Onda is a cozy-gaming community app where you share game spaces in the Village, arrange in-game item trades, exchange letters with friends, host and join Tea Time gatherings, and keep a personal Teahouse brewing journal.

  • App name: Onda
  • Bundle ID: com.onda.olrt
  • Contact: OndaEnterprise@gamil.com

By using Onda you agree to this Privacy Policy and to our Terms of Service.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Account information

Onda uses Quick Login. When you first sign in, the app creates a username (for example cozy_ab12cd) and a random password for your account and saves them in the app's storage on your iPhone. We also record the time you confirmed you are 18 or older and agreed to the Terms of Service. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or date of birth to sign in.

1.2 Profile information you choose to add

During onboarding and on the Edit Cozy Card screen you may add a display name, avatar, bio, favorite games, cozy type, circles, a "playing now" status, and gaming handles such as a Switch Friend Code, an Island Pass or a PC tag. These handles are optional. Share them only if you want other players to be able to find you in your games.

1.3 Content you create

  • Village: Spaces (photos, captions, circles, voice space notes), Trades (item photos, titles, descriptions, what you want in return), likes, notes and comments.
  • Letters: messages, photos and voice notes you send in letter chats, and the stationery you pick.
  • Tea Time: gatherings you host or join, tea room chat messages, scrapbook memories, reactions and notes.
  • Teahouse: brew sessions, tasting journal entries, your game shelf (titles, notes and an optional cover photo), postcards, weekly challenges and Cupboard items you unlock.

This content is saved in Onda's app storage on your iPhone so it appears in your feed, chats and journal.

1.4 Petals and purchases

We keep your Petals balance, the Petals you spend (for example on hosting a Tea Time, Teahouse Cupboard items or letter stationery), and the IDs of App Store transactions already credited, so a purchase is never credited twice.

1.5 Safety records

When you report, hide or block, Onda keeps a record of the reported item, the reason you chose, any note you wrote, the content you removed from your feed and the users you blocked. These records keep the hidden content out of your feed. See Section 6.

1.6 What we do not collect

Onda does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not collect your precise location, contacts or health data.

2. How we use information

We use the information above only to run Onda's features:

  • To show your profile, posts, trades, letters, gatherings and journal
  • To filter text before you publish (see Section 6)
  • To credit Petals after a verified App Store purchase and to record what you spend
  • To schedule reminders you request, such as the Teahouse "tea is ready" alert
  • To respond when you contact us or send a report

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Facial data

4.1 What we collect

Onda does not collect facial data. We do not use Face ID data, TrueDepth camera data, ARKit face tracking, facial landmarks, facial geometry, face embeddings or any biometric identifier, and we do not run face detection or face recognition on any image.

You may choose to add photos (for example an avatar, a Space photo, a Trade photo or a letter photo) that happen to show a face. Onda treats these as ordinary images and does not analyze them for facial features.

4.2 How we use it

Photos you add are used only to display them in the place you put them: your Cozy Card avatar, a Space or Trade post, a letter chat, a shelf cover or a postcard. They are never used to identify you, to verify identity, for advertising or to train models.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

We do not disclose, sell or share facial data, because we do not collect any. Photos you attach are not sent to any third-party service, including any AI or face-analysis provider. A photo is shown to other people only when you post it in the Village, a letter or a tea room yourself.

4.4 Storage and retention

Photos you attach are saved in Onda's app storage on your iPhone (the OndaMedia folder in the app's Documents directory). They are kept until you delete the post or message they belong to, or until you delete your account, which removes them at once. Images you save to your photo library on purpose are managed by you in the Photos app.

4.5 Scope of this section

This Section 4 is Onda's complete explanation of what facial data the app collects, how it is used, whether it is disclosed or shared, where it is stored, and how long it is kept.

5. Third-party AI services

Onda does not use any third-party artificial intelligence or machine-learning service. No prompts, photos, audio, text or other user data are sent to any AI provider. Voice-to-Text Caption uses Apple's built-in Speech framework (Section 3), not a third-party AI service.

6. Community safety: filtering, reports, hide and block

Onda follows a zero-tolerance policy for objectionable content and abusive users (see the Community Standards in our Terms of Service).

  • Filtering: Before you publish a Space, Trade, note, comment, letter or tea room message, Onda checks the text against a blocklist of abusive, sexual and hateful terms, outside links, and real-money payment words. If something matches, it is not published until you edit it.
  • Report: You can report a post, trade, comment, letter or user from the "more" menu on a card or from the detail screen. The reported item is hidden from your feed right away and its author is removed from your view. Onda then opens your Mail app with a pre-filled email to OndaEnterprise@gamil.com containing a ticket number, the reason, the reported username, the content ID, a short snippet of the content (up to 280 characters) and your optional note. The email is sent only if you tap Send, and it is sent from your own email account.
  • Remove from feed: Hides a single item from your feed.
  • Block: After you confirm twice, all content from that user is hidden and they can no longer appear in your feed or letters.
  • Delete: You can delete your own Spaces, Trades and comments at any time.

We review every report email and act within 24 hours, as described in the Terms of Service.

7. In-app purchases

Petals are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes all payments. We never receive or store your card number, billing address or Apple ID password. We receive only the information StoreKit provides to confirm a purchase (the product ID and transaction ID), which we use to credit the right amount of Petals. Purchases are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Data retention and deleting your account

Your information is kept while you use Onda.

  • Log Out signs you out but keeps your account so you can sign back in with Quick Login.
  • Delete Account (Me > Delete Account, then type DELETE) permanently erases your account details, profile and Cozy Card, posts, trades, comments, letters, tea gatherings and scrapbook entries, Teahouse journal, shelf, postcards and Cupboard items, photos and voice notes, Petals balance and purchase records, report, hide and block lists, and settings. Any pending Teahouse reminder is cancelled. This cannot be undone, and unused Petals are not refunded.

Emails you sent us (reports or support requests) are kept only as long as needed to handle them. To ask for these to be deleted, email us.

9. Children's privacy

Onda is intended only for users aged 18 and over. You must confirm you are 18 or older before signing in. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using Onda, contact us and we will act on it.

10. Security

Onda relies on iOS app sandboxing and data protection to secure the information stored by the app. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device protected with a passcode.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to some uses of it. You can edit your profile in the app, delete any content you posted, or delete your account at any time. For any other request, email us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Onda changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will let you know in the app. Continuing to use Onda after a change means you accept the updated policy.
